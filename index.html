Application of CDR (Competency Demonstration Report)

Migration

CDR is required for skills assessment of engineers by Engineer Australia for skilled immigration visa approval

Accreditation

A well prepared CDR is mandatory for stage 2 skills assessment of engineer to get a CPEng (Chartered Engineer) and other accreditations.

Registration

Get put the name of an engineer in various engineering registers in Australia like NER (National Engineering Register), an engineer has to go through skills assessment by authorized assessment agencies like EA or BPEQ.
